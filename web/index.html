HOW THE FARE WORKS

Every rupee, explained.

Base fare covers the first kilometres, then two per-km slabs, a per-minute ride charge and a small booking fee. Surge is capped at 3× and always shown. Night rides (11pm–5am) carry a 1.25× on distance & time. Waiting past 5 minutes is billed per minute. Prime members skip surge entirely and save 10%. GST on top — and that's it. The app shows this exact breakdown before you book.
