faq

quick answers ⚡

How is the fare calculated?

Base fare + per-km + per-minute for your chosen category, shown upfront before you book. Tolls and taxes included. The price you accept is the price you pay.

Can I schedule a ride in advance?

Yes — schedule any ride up to 7 days ahead. We assign a driver ~15 minutes before pickup and notify you.

What payment methods work?

UPI, credit/debit cards, netbanking, RYDR Wallet and good old cash. You can also split the fare with friends in-app.

How do I become a driver partner?

Download the partner app, upload your license, RC and ID, pass a quick background check, and you're on the road — usually within 48 hours.

What if I leave something in the car?

Open the ride in History → "Lost item" and we'll connect you to your driver over a masked call immediately.
